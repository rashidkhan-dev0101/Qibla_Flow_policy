# Privacy Policy - Qibla Flow

Developer: Rashid Khan

Qibla Flow respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles your information.

## 1. Information We Collect
We do NOT collect, store, or share any personal data.

## 2. Location Usage
The app uses your device’s location only to calculate the direction of Qibla.

Your location data is:
- Used only on your device
- NOT stored on any server
- NOT shared with any third party

## 3. Permissions
The app may request the following permission:

- Location Permission: Required to determine Qibla direction accurately.

## 4. Data Security
Since we do not collect or store any personal data, your information remains secure on your device.

These services may collect:

## 6. Age Restriction
Qibla Flow is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older.
The app is not directed toward children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
